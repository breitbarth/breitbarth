# Sales Résumé System

By Aaron Breitbarth

A repeatable way to turn sales work into a résumé a hiring manager can scan and an interview story you can defend. Use it for outbound sales, account executive, and sales leadership applications.

## 1. Build an evidence bank

Keep this worksheet private. Document a single result per row and point to whatever supports it.

| Role and period | Problem or goal | What I did | Result and scope | How I checked it | Skill it proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Role, month or quarter] | [Target, stalled pipeline, or coverage gap] | [Decision or action I owned] | [Outcome, unit, baseline, timeframe] | [Sales report, quota dashboard, or attributable feedback] | [Prospecting, closing, coaching, or territory planning] |

Separate individual production from team production. Record what the number counts, such as signed accounts, activated customers, qualified meetings, or booked revenue. If you cannot verify a number, describe the action and observable outcome in plain language.

## 2. Match the posting to your evidence

| If the posting emphasizes | Lead with | Follow with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outbound sales | Personally sourced meetings, accounts, or pipeline | Prospecting channels, qualification, and follow-up |
| Account executive | Closed revenue or quota attainment | Discovery, demos, negotiation, and closing |
| Sales leadership | Team attainment, headcount, and timeframe | Coaching, hiring, territory decisions, and how you tracked progress |

Pick the three to five requirements you can actually demonstrate. Use the employer's wording where it accurately describes your work.

## 3. Write the bullet and prepare the interview story

**Résumé formula:** Action you owned + what you did differently + outcome with context.

**Interview formula:** Problem → process → concrete example → impact → how you confirmed it → skill the employer needs.

Use the full interview formula in your notes. The résumé bullet only needs enough detail to earn the conversation.

### Worked examples

These examples use fictional employers, situations, and numbers.

- **Outbound sales:** Booked 18 qualified meetings in one quarter by sorting local business accounts by renewal month and following up with owners before contracts expired.
- **Account executive:** Closed $180,000 in annual recurring revenue in the second quarter by running discovery with buyers and aligning proposals with their renewal dates.
- **Sales leadership:** Led nine field representatives to 108 activations against a monthly target of 90 (120%). Used service-availability updates to assign newly eligible addresses and coached reps on follow-up in the field.

For the leadership example, the interview story would start with reps working stale address lists, describe how you reassigned the newest eligible blocks, then explain how you checked the activation report against the team target. The résumé may split quota results and territory decisions into two bullets if you cannot substantiate a causal link between them.

## 5. Review before submitting

- [ ] In the first ten seconds, a reader can tell what you sell, how you sell it, and whether your results are individual or team results.
- [ ] Every headline metric has a unit, period, and source in your private notes. Quota attainment equals actual results divided by target; 108 against 90 is 120% of target.
- [ ] Titles are accurate. If the internal title is unclear, add a descriptor such as “(Direct Sales).”
- [ ] Dates and employers are easy to follow. Stack roles at the same employer when that makes the progression clearer.
- [ ] Body text starts around 11 points. Edit for relevance before shrinking type.
- [ ] Export a Portable Document Format (PDF) copy and check page breaks, links, and selectable text. Keep the editable master and submit the file type the employer requests.
- [ ] Before sharing a public example, remove personal contact details, confidential figures, and document metadata. Replace real details with clearly labeled fictional examples.

Keep one master evidence bank. For each application, save a tailored copy and record which job it was sent to. Update the master when you verify a new result, then reuse that evidence across future applications.
