# Proposed experiment: representation and transfer

Randomly assign consenting adult learners to equivalent symbolic or spatial explanations. Match instructional time and content; measure baseline knowledge. Test immediate and delayed performance on novel problems without instructional aids. Score explanations using a predefined rubric with blinded scoring where feasible. Record accessibility needs, missing data and uncertainty. Predefine sample size and analysis before recruitment; obtain appropriate ethics review where required.

Primary outcome: novel-problem accuracy. Secondary: explanation quality, retention and time. Hypothesis: representation affects transfer; null and adverse effects remain possible. No participants recruited or results obtained.

## Candidate task: odd-number invariant

Compare equivalent symbolic and spatial-layer explanations of the odd-number sum identity. Use separate discovery, near-transfer and far-transfer items; for example, predict the difference between consecutive square totals, then derive a related rectangular growth rule. Record prior exposure, explanation rubric and time; do not use the open public demo as randomized evidence. Its controls allow disclosure of the rule, so it cannot distinguish independent discovery from reading the explanation.
