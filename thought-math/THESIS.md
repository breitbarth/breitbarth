# Working thesis

Mathematical objects and truth conditions may be independent of sensory modality while access, discovery and understanding depend on representation. This is a hypothesis and philosophical position, not an established finding.

Can artificial intelligence discover new mathematics and also create representations that expand human mathematical thinking?

Distinguish formal topology from metaphor: when claiming invariance, specify objects, mappings and preserved relations. A representation is useful when it supports independent reasoning beyond the original example.

Original project direction: Aaron Anthony Breitbarth. AI-assisted elaboration; no claim of priority over existing mathematical cognition research.
