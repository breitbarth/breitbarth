# Working thesis

Mathematical objects and truth conditions may be independent of sensory modality while access, discovery and understanding depend on representation. This is a hypothesis and philosophical position, not an established finding.

Can artificial intelligence discover new mathematics and also create representations that expand human mathematical thinking?

Distinguish formal topology from metaphor: when claiming invariance, specify objects, mappings and preserved relations. A representation is useful when it supports independent reasoning beyond the original example.

Original project direction: Aaron Anthony Breitbarth. AI-assisted elaboration; no claim of priority over existing mathematical cognition research.

## Representation, discovery and invariance

Working distinction: encoding communicates an already known relation; discovery exposes a relation not previously recognized; transfer applies it beyond the original representation. These are proposed analytical categories, not established psychological constructs.

A concrete example: the first n odd integers sum to n². A symbolic sum, running totals and growing L-shaped square borders encode the same relationship, but the border construction exposes why the increment is 2n−1. The proof uses disjoint layers of sizes 1, 3, 5, …, 2n−1. The representation does not change the mathematical truth; it changes which structure is directly accessible.

Research question: can representation support discovery, explanation and far transfer beyond memorized procedures? Compare competing representations against equivalent content and time, accounting for prior familiarity. Do not equate subjective aha reports with understanding.
