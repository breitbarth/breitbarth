# Research agenda

- Cognitive topology: define representation spaces and mappings; identify preserved relations and translation residue.
- Mathematical representation: compare symbolic, graphical and narrative access; distinguish abstraction from presentation.
- Sensory abstraction: investigate tactile-first and auditory representations without assuming disability confers special abilities.
- Organizational psychology: examine whether alternative representations of feedback and incentives facilitate transfer to new management problems.
- Human–AI collaboration: test whether generated representations expose previously unnoticed relationships.

Separate formal claims, empirical hypotheses and analogies. Record counterexamples and negative results.
