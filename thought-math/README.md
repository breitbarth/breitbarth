# Thought Math
*Mathematics for the machinery between your ears.*

A research initiative originated by Aaron Anthony Breitbarth, with AI-assisted drafting. The sensory topology of mathematics was discussed September 4, 2026; the Thought Math working definition was articulated October 2, 2026.

**Working definition:** the disciplined study of how minds represent relationships—and how those representations expand what minds can think.

## Process
Translate a relationship across symbolic, spatial, tactile, auditory and narrative representations; identify what survives translation; test independent transfer.

## Problem
Correct answers do not necessarily establish understanding. Representations may affect which relationships learners can discover and use.

## Example
Compare multiplication as notation, an array and grouped objects; then test a novel problem without the original aids.

## Impact
Potential applications include education, accessible interfaces and human–AI collaboration. No experimental effects have been established.

## Confirm
Measure novel-problem accuracy, explanation quality, retention and transfer, not merely preference.
