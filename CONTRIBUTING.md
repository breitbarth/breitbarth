# Contributing

Questions, counterexamples, corrections and reproducible examples are welcome via GitHub issues where enabled. Identify the project and version, describe the observation, separate empirical evidence from speculation, and cite relevant sources. Do not include customer data, credentials, personal records or confidential material.

For proposed changes explain: problem, example, intended impact, how to confirm, limitations. Retain attribution to Aaron Breitbarth for documented contributions and credit other contributors and upstream sources separately. Do not infer originality from publication date.

Public access is not blanket permission to copy, redistribute or adapt the material. Licensing decisions are pending author review; obtain permission where needed. Contributions do not imply a license grant, acceptance or collaboration agreement. See [AI usage](AI_USAGE.md) and [project standards](PROJECT_STANDARD.md).
