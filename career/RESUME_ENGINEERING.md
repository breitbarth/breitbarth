# Resume engineering methodology

Author and maintainer: Aaron Breitbarth. Working process, not a validated hiring intervention.

## Process
Role requirements → evidence inventory → relevance ranking → draft → verification → formatting → export → outcome review.

## Problem
Generic resumes hide relevant strengths; automated tailoring can introduce unsupported claims.

## Evidence inventory
For each accomplishment record role, timeframe, action, metric definition, baseline, outcome, source, confidence and confidentiality. Keep source documents private.

## Relevance formula
Suggested prioritization score = 0.40 role relevance + 0.30 evidence strength + 0.20 business impact + 0.10 recency. Score each input 0–5. This is a proposed heuristic, not a hiring prediction.

## Bullet construction
Action + scope + method + verified result. Example: Led regional field sales operations and delivered 224 sales against a September target of 160 (140% attainment). Verify the metric and period before submission.

## Formatting and quality gate
- Put the strongest relevant evidence near the top.
- Use readable typography, consistent dates and clear headings.
- Prefer standard section names and simple layouts for applicant tracking systems.
- Inspect the exported PDF visually and verify text extraction and page breaks.
- Match keywords truthfully; do not fabricate experience or credentials.
- Check every number against the evidence inventory.
- Retain a version per role and log response and interview outcomes.

## Confirm
Evaluate qualified application response and interview conversion, time spent, and factual error rate. Compare within similar role categories; avoid attributing changes to formatting alone.

## AI assistance
Use artificial intelligence to propose variants, identify gaps and improve clarity. The applicant verifies facts and owns the final submission. See [AI principles](../AI_USAGE.md).

## Relevant features
Evidence ledger, role scoring, bullet construction, formatting checks, version control and outcome feedback.
