# Job Search Operating System

**Stage: documented workflow; outcome validation pending.**

## Process
1. Define target roles, compensation, geography and constraints.
2. Source opportunities and assess recruiter legitimacy.
3. Score fit against actual requirements and verified experience.
4. Tailor the resume using an evidence inventory, without inventing qualifications.
5. Research employer, buyer, product and interview participants using appropriate public sources.
6. Prepare relevant sales stories, discovery questions and objection responses.
7. Log applications, follow-ups, interviews and outcomes.
8. Review conversion by source and stage weekly; revise the approach.

## Problem
Application volume alone obscures fit, conversion and the quality of follow-through.

## Example
For a revenue operations role, connect team leadership, territory coordination and analytics experience to the job requirements. Record gaps explicitly.

## Impact
Intended: consistent applications, better preparation and measurable learning. Hiring improvements have not been established.

## Confirm
Track qualified applications, response rate, interview rate, offers and time spent. Compare by source and role; avoid causal claims from small samples.

## Relevant features
- Evidence-based resume tailoring
- Recruiter verification
- Interview preparation
- Pipeline tracking and retrospective
