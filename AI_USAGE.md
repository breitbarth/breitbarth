# AI-assisted development principles

These are working standards for human-directed development, not a claim that every historical repository followed them.

1. Define the problem, users, requirements and constraints.
2. Use artificial intelligence to explore alternatives and assist implementation.
3. Review generated work for accuracy, security, licensing and fitness.
4. Test against explicit acceptance criteria and retain reproducible evidence.
5. Attribute original ideas, third-party work and AI-assisted contributions accurately.
6. Document limitations and avoid unsupported claims.

The maintainer is responsible for deciding what to publish. Never publish private information or proprietary concepts simply to strengthen a portfolio.
