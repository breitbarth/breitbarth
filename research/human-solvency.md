# Human Solvency and Resource Revaluation

Author and originator: Aaron Anthony Breitbarth. AI-assisted research note. Published November 20, 2026.

## Process
Examine how technological expansion changes resource constraints and how human capacity interacts with those constraints.

## Problem
Economic capacity may be overstated when physical infrastructure is measured without considering human and institutional ability to use it.

## Example
Computing capacity depends on power, cooling, materials, geography, permission and people capable of maintaining and governing infrastructure.

## Proposed hypothesis
Measures of workforce health, financial resilience and institutional capacity may predict operational continuity beyond physical capital alone. Human solvency is a proposed multidimensional construct, not a financial or clinical diagnosis.

## Impact
Potential applications: resource economics, industrial geography and organizational resilience.

## Confirm
Define measurable indicators separately, test incremental predictive value against a capital-only baseline, use held-out observations and account for confounding, reverse causation and unequal measurement. Do not infer individual worth from productivity. No empirical results yet.

## Relevant features
Complementary inputs, bottleneck shifts, human capital and resilience. Literature review leads: capabilities approach, human capital, infrastructure economics and ecological economics.
