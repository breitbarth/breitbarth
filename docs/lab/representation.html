Thought Math

Mathematics for the machinery between your ears.

Can changing the form of a relationship make a previously hidden structure visible? Explore, predict, then explain. These are guided illustrations, not validated cognitive assessments.

01 / The hidden square

Consider the sum of successive odd numbers. What happens as you add another?

Reveal the invariant

The first n positive odd numbers sum to n². Each new L-shaped border adds 2n−1 cells, transforming an (n−1) × (n−1) square into an n × n square.

02 / Transfer without the diagram

The first 12 positive odd numbers sum to what? What is the difference between that total and the sum of the first 11?

Your written explanation is not automatically scored or transmitted.
