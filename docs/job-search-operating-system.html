Job Search Operating System

The workflow I use to select roles, tailor a résumé to evidence, prepare for interviews, and learn from the results.

Aaron Breitbarth

From a posting to a decision

  1. Define the search. Set target roles, compensation, geography, and other constraints.
  2. Check the opportunity. Find the employer’s posting and verify who is recruiting.
  3. Score the fit. Match the requirements to verified experience and record the gaps.
  4. Tailor the résumé. Select accomplishments that support the role and retain the underlying evidence.
  5. Research the interview. Learn about the employer, buyer, product, and interview participants.
  6. Prepare examples. Choose relevant sales stories, discovery questions, and responses to likely objections.
  7. Log the next action. Track applications, follow-ups, interviews, and outcomes.
  8. Review weekly. Compare progress by source and stage, then adjust the search.

How I would assess a revenue operations role

For a role that asks for territory planning and reporting, I would map field leadership, territory coordination, and analytics work to those requirements. If the employer asks for a tool or responsibility I cannot demonstrate, I record the gap before choosing the résumé examples.

What I track

Qualified applications, response rate, interview rate, offers, and time spent show where an application process needs attention. Small samples call for caution when drawing conclusions about why a stage improved. The workflow is documented and its hiring outcomes still need validation.
